ETDP AI Learning Design System Transforming vocational education through accredited 
automation

The ETDP AI Learning Design System is a pioneering platform that redefines vocational learning 
programme design. By scanning official QCTO curricula and assessment specifications, it generates 
complete suites of accredited learning resources ² including learner guides, assessments, roll-out 
plans, logbooks, and lecturer toolkits ² in under 40 minutes. This innovation compresses months of 
preparation into minutes, ensuring efficiency, integrity, and compliance.

The ETDP AI Learning Design System is more than a tool ² it is an infrastructure for educational 
transformation. It empowers institutions to deliver accredited, compliant, and globally competitive 
programmes while restoring focus to pedagogy and learner success.

The Education and Training Learning Program Design System is an integrated digital platform for 
designing, organising, generating, reviewing, and exporting accredited learning programmes. The 
system supports the full curriculum development journey, from qualification setup and Quality Council 
curriculum uploads through to lesson planning, learner registration, work experience records, 
assessment material, learning schedules, workbooks, memoranda, progress reports, slide decks, flow 
diagrams, and final export packs.

At its centre, the system helps training providers turn formal curriculum and assessment specifications 
into practical teaching, learning, and assessment documents. It keeps the qualification structure 
organised by phases, subjects, topics, outcomes, lesson plan content, and learning material outputs. It 
also provides guided menus, workflow checks, preview screens, export functions, upload templates, 
diagnostics, and AI-assisted support so that curriculum development can be completed in a more 
consistent, traceable, and efficient way.

The platform is designed for education and training environments where accuracy, version control, audit 
readiness, and repeatable document generation are important. Users can upload official curriculum 
documents, import structured templates, review mapped content, generate draft learning and assessment 
material with the configured AI specialist service, and then download or save the resulting documents 
for moderation, printing, or institutional use. Although the system automates much of the drafting and 
formatting work, final academic review and approval remain the responsibility of the training provider.
